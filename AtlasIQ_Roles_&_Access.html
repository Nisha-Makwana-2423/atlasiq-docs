AtlasIQ · Organization tenants

CQH (Constellation Quality Health) is one tenant. The same role structure applies to other tenants, such as BQH or AQH. Each tenant has its own staff, providers, customers, external partners and platform workspace.

Customers and programs belong within a tenant. Access stays within that tenant and is further limited by customer, program and role.

The CQH tenant is shown below with 14 roles across five groups. Everyone signs in at the same place, and what they sign in with decides which workspace opens and what they can reach. Pick a role to see what it does, how it signs in, its screens, its access to each kind of record and every requirement it appears in.

How this was builtThe organization-tenant layer reflects the confirmed hierarchy update. Roles and their descriptions come from AtlasIQ Estimation Requirements doc (566 req) section 2.3. The roles linked to each requirement are the actors named in AtlasIQ Estimation Requirements doc (566 req) section 3. Screens come from the Final Scope workbook, and the journey comes from the Process Diagrams.
Stated vs inferredThe AtlasIQ Estimation Requirements doc (566 req) does not contain a permission matrix. Access marked stated is written in the AtlasIQ Estimation Requirements doc (566 req). Access marked inferred (dashed border) follows from the role's duties and should be confirmed before build.
GroupingReviewer, Indexing Team, Doc Eval Team, Letters Team and Training Staff are shown as specialised internal users under Internal User. The AtlasIQ Estimation Requirements doc (566 req) lists each as its own role.
